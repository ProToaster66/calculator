Requirements

The calculator must be able to:

1: be able to add two numbers together
2: subtract one number from another
3: multiply two numbers together
4: divide two numbers from one another
5: display the result

The calculator must be able to meet all of these reauirements to be considered done.

Examples of inputs and expected outputs include: 

2 + 8 = 10

5 * 10 = 50

25 / 5 = 5
